capture 30 — GROUND, GLASS AND WEATHER: the field rolls now (a real displaced mesh with grass, dead grass, scoured earth and mossy hollows blended across it, and men standing on the contour); windows are twelve-light sashes with sills, muntins and shutters instead of coloured boxes; walls carry soot streaks, damp courses and failed paint; roofs vary in pitch and age, and houses grew dormers and lean-tos.
