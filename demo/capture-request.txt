capture 32 — WHO IS WHO, AND HOW IT IS LIT: every company now has an officer with a sword and no musket, a sergeant whose spontoon tops the muskets, a drummer in reversed facings, and the colours on a pole above everything — men you can name at two hundred yards by silhouette. Five dressings across the ranks instead of three. And a proper light rig: warm key, cool fill, a bounce off the earth into the undersides of coats and hat brims, a rim to lift dark figures off dark ground, and shadows with soft edges.
