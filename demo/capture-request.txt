capture 19 — REALISM PASS: men who move, and men who differ. Marching legs and counter-swinging arms, muskets to the present, ramrods working, chargers leaning in. Rounded limbs and tapered coats instead of stacked boxes, tricorns with three cocked corners, hats and kit and cloth that vary man to man. Dawn light and mist on Lexington Green, depth fog carrying the distance.
